# 60-second demo script (Space Apps video)

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Home page, Video mode | "This is the Earth Information Jukebox. It turns NASA Earth videos and maps into sound, so people who cannot see them can hear them." |
| 0:08–0:22 | Video → Air Quality (PM2.5) → **Generate sound** → **Play**. The line moves left to right over the PM2.5 map. | "This is NASA's PM2.5 air-quality video. The line scans left to right. Darker, dustier air sounds louder and rougher, and the sound moves from the left ear to the right. Listen as it crosses Asia." |
| 0:22–0:38 | Click **Picture** → Temperature (Sea Surface) → **Generate** → **Play**. The yellow box zig-zags; the values table highlights the band. | "Picture mode reads the official legend. Each strip plays three notes, north, middle and south. Cold water is a low note, warm water a high note. The table and the caption always show the exact value range." |
| 0:38–0:48 | Click **Download video**, show the progress bar, then open the saved MP4 (picture + line + sound). | "Any result can be downloaded as a video with sound, or as a WAV file. Everything is made in the browser; nothing is uploaded." |
| 0:48–0:60 | Keyboard only: Tab to the switch, **G** to generate, **Space** to play, **←/→** to step the line, **M** to mute. Show the live caption. | "It works fully from the keyboard and with screen readers, with a live caption once per second. Hear the planet." |

Tip: open `…/#picture` to start directly in Picture mode.
