    The Sonification Architecture

    How satellite imagery becomes spatial sound without uploading files to any server.

    1. Spatial Dissection

    Each video frame or map is partitioned into 32 vertical columns and 3 latitudinal tiers (96 distinct acoustic sectors). Color arrays are extracted client-side using offscreen canvases.

    2. Legend Calibration

    Colors are matched in perceptual CIELAB space against official NASA GIBS scientific palettes, mapping directly to physical units: degrees Celsius, millimeters per hour, AOD, and NDVI.

    3. Binaural Synthesis

    Dedicated Web Workers run pure DSP engines with biquad filters, seeded stochastic noise, and envelope smoothers, panning sound across stereo channels in sync with the visual scan.

    Earth Information Jukebox is an open-source sonification project for NASA Space Apps Challenge #14. Visuals and data: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center (GMAO, SVS, Worldview/GIBS).

    All audio synthesis runs 100% locally in your browser via Web Audio and Web Workers.
