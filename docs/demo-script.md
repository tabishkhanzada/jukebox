# 🎤 The Earth Information Jukebox — Hackathon Demo Script

**Event:** NASA Space Apps Challenge  
**Target Duration:** 90–120 seconds  
**Presenters:** Team Lead / Audio Engineer / Storyteller  

---

## 🎬 Video & Demo Flow

### 0:00 – 0:15 | The Hook (The Problem)
- **Visual:** Open on the Earth Information Jukebox dashboard (`http://127.0.0.1:8000/`).
- **Speaker:** 
  > *"Every day, NASA satellites capture gigabytes of critical climate data. But for most people, long spreadsheets and complex 2D graphs are dry, hard to interpret, and completely inaccessible to visually impaired individuals. What if you didn't have to read climate change — what if you could hear it?"*

### 0:15 – 0:40 | The Solution & Live Playback (Karachi Heatwave)
- **Action:** Select **Karachi (2024)**, **Temperature**, and click the large glowing **PLAY** button.
- **Audio:** The melody starts in low, serene notes in winter (January), gently rises into spring, and reaches high, sparkling frequencies during the June heatwave peak (33.5°C), while soft monthly sub-bass pulses mark calendar progression.
- **Visual:** The full 3D glowing orange Earth sphere rotates in space, emitting holographic radar shockwaves outward from the station reticle across the continents with every musical beat. The 5-column telemetry HUD decrypts live weather, acoustic pitch, and season data.
- **Speaker:**
  > *"Meet The Earth Information Jukebox. Here, daily satellite telemetry from the NASA Langley POWER Project is quantized into harmonious pentatonic scales. Watch our 3D solar Earth sphere: as daily temperatures modulate upward during the summer heatwave, the melody transposes higher and holographic acoustic shockwaves ripple outward from the station pin, making extreme climate anomalies instantly intuitive."*

### 0:40 – 1:05 | Dual-Track Accompaniment (Adding Rainfall Drums)
- **Action:** Toggle on the **"Dual-Track (Layer Rain Beat)"** switch.
- **Audio:** In August, as the monsoon hits, distinct percussive drum strikes fire in sync with precipitation peaks while the temperature melody plays above it.
- **Visual:** The chart dynamically renders rainfall bars behind the temperature curve.
- **Speaker:**
  > *"With our dual-track engine, we layer the climate dimensions together. Temperature plays the melody, while rainfall triggers percussive acoustic strikes. Watch and listen to August: as the monsoon downpour begins, resonant drumbeats enter the song, mirroring the rainfall spikes on the dual-axis chart."*

### 1:05 – 1:20 | Global Exploration & Export
- **Action:** Switch city to **London** or **Tokyo**, hit play, show the contrast in melody. Click **"Download Audio"** to demonstrate recording, and **"Export CSV"**.
- **Speaker:**
  > *"Users can inspect presets from London to Tokyo or input custom latitude and longitude coordinates. Students and researchers can export cleaned telemetry CSVs or record and download the generated audio piece directly in their browser."*

### 1:20 – 1:30 | Closing & Impact
- **Speaker:**
  > *"Built with FastAPI, Tone.js, and Chart.js, The Earth Information Jukebox bridges science, music, and accessibility — letting the world listen to the pulse of our living planet. Thank you!"*
